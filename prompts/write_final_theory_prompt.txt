2. **Model.** The exact framework actually used, per MAIN_PLAN.md's final specification.

## Ground rules

- Every claim in theory.tex must be traceable: either to a specific note/data file in `main_derivation/`, or to a specific literature citation verified via a `search-literature` dispatch — never cite a paper from memory.
- If two notes disagree, or a later note supersedes an earlier one, resolve it using the LATER note's conclusion.
- Do not pad, but do not compress either — this must be complete enough that a reader who has never seen `main_derivation/` could reproduce the result from theory.tex + `tools/` + the cited literature alone. Write it in the style of a typical theoretical derivation.
