## Breadth first, not depth first

`EXPLORE_PLAN.md` lists several candidate approaches on purpose — the point of this stage is to find out which one is worth committing to, not to fully develop the first one that starts producing interesting results. Any numerical stress-test approach exists to inform that choice. Producing a clean, well-verified result from one approach's first layer is a sign to move on and check the others. Writing an in-depth, complete theory is not appropriate at this stage.

Before you spend more time deepening any one approach past the "first-layer" budget the EXPLORE_PLAN.md describes for it, stop and check that you have indeed evaluated the other approaches.

When you do decide to stop deepening an approach (whether it looks promising or not), log that explicitly as a `decision` action, citing the specific criterion from `EXPLORE_PLAN.md` that triggered it, before moving to the next one.
